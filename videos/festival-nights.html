Festival Nights on FlexTram

After dark at a music festival: riders loading at a covered tram station and full trains heading out under the lights.

Published November 17, 2026 · 1:17 · Watch on YouTube

What the video shows

This 77-second film is set entirely after dark at a music festival. It opens at a covered tram station strung with lights, where riders wait behind barricades and board as each train pulls in, then follows full trains of festival-goers out across the grounds under the stage and tower lighting.

Festivals are where the gap between a golf cart and a system is widest. One FlexTram train carries what a whole row of carts would, on one driver, and a station gives the crowd one place to wait instead of a scramble at the curb. The film shows the hardest hour of the night: the end of the show, when everyone wants to leave at once.

Scene by scene

  • A covered tram station at night, strung with lights, with riders queued behind barricades.
  • Full trains pulling into the station and loading.
  • Trains moving across the festival grounds under colored stage lighting.
  • Light-trail shots of trains in motion.
  • The FlexTram name over the station crowd.
