FlexTram x LAZ Live! at NASCAR San Diego Weekend 2026

FlexTram and LAZ Live! moving race fans between the lots and the event at NASCAR San Diego Weekend 2026.

Published November 2, 2026 · 1:20 · Watch on YouTube

What the video shows

Shot at NASCAR San Diego Weekend 2026, this 80-second film shows FlexTram running fan transport with LAZ Live!, from LAZ Parking. Trams loop between the parking areas and the event along the fenced race-weekend perimeter, stopping at numbered tram stop markers while LAZ staff in high-visibility vests load riders.

It is the parking-operator model in practice. The operator runs the lots, the staffing, and the customer relationship, and FlexTram supplies the vehicles and the route plan for the stretch between the car and the gate. We wrote about that model in Your parking operator is already running half a transit system, and about what it does to the operator’s contract in The 30-day problem.

Scene by scene

  • The fan services gate and the FlexTram x LAZ title.
  • Race cars on pit road behind the fence.
  • The NASCAR San Diego Weekend branding.
  • Riders boarding covered tram cars on the paved lot.
  • A flyover by a parachute team trailing smoke.
  • A numbered tram stop marker for stops 1 and 2.
  • LAZ Parking staff at the loading point beside a tow vehicle.
  • Full trains pulling away across the lot toward the event.
