Common questions

What does FlexTram look like in operation?

These videos show FlexTram at real events: a full venue day from 06:00 gates-open to 23:10 egress, fan transport with LAZ Live! at NASCAR San Diego Weekend 2026, and a music festival after dark. Each train is a tow vehicle pulling covered tram cars, run by one driver.

How many people does a FlexTram carry?

Up to 27 seated passengers per tram, with one driver. The venue video shows trains loading 27 riders a trip.

Is FlexTram ADA compliant?

Yes. ADA compliance is standard on every FlexTram, not an add-on.

Where does FlexTram operate?

FlexTram deploys throughout the United States and Canada, at festivals, stadiums, motorsports venues, airports, cruise terminals, data centers, and corporate and university campuses.

Is FlexTram on YouTube?

Yes. All of these videos are on the FlexTramHQ YouTube channel.
