FlexTram for Venues

A full venue day on FlexTram, from 06:00 gates-open to 23:10 egress: 27 riders a trip, on the paths the venue already has.

Published November 12, 2026 · 1:22 · Watch on YouTube

What the video shows

This 82-second film follows a venue day from first arrival to last departure. It opens on a race day with 40,000 fans on the way and the long walk they face from the lots. Then it cuts to the operation: FlexTram trains loading at the gate from 06:00, 27 riders a trip, running on the service roads and paths the venue already has.

The second half moves to night. At 23:10 everyone leaves at once, and the trams load from covered pickup stations under string lights while the drivers keep the line moving. The film closes on an empty lot the next morning: “By morning, your lot is yours again.”

The footage comes from FlexTram deployments at motorsports, festival, and college football events, including Bonnaroo, which the film names on screen. The logo sequence shows venues and partners FlexTram has worked with: NASCAR, Daytona International Speedway, Live Nation, AEG, LAZ Parking, Smart Financial Centre at Sugar Land, the University of Louisville, and Florida State University.

Scene by scene

  • “Race day. 40,000 fans on the way.” Crowds at the barricades outside a speedway.
  • “They’ll make that walk.” A long perimeter path along the fence line.
  • Logos of venues and partners FlexTram has worked with.
  • “06:00 Gates open. 27 riders a trip.” Trains loading fans at the gate.
  • “Runs on the paths you already have.” Trains on service roads, gravel, and grass.
  • “High capacity,” with a ride count from a single day at Bonnaroo.
  • “23:10 Egress. Everyone leaves at once.” Night loading at a covered station.
  • “Our drivers.” Full trains pulling out under festival lights.
  • “By morning, your lot is yours again.” An empty lot the next day.
  • “Built in the U.S.” and “We’re built to move.”
