Avoid the Wilderness with FlexTram

A 28-second spot: getting around an event shouldn’t feel like wandering in the wilderness.

Published April 21, 2026 · 0:28 · Watch on YouTube

What the video shows

A short animated spot. The line on screen reads “Attending an event… shouldn’t feel like wandering in the wilderness,” next to a FlexTram stop sign marked Blue Line, stop 2, pick up and drop off. It answers with “We make you feel like you’ve arrived,” cuts to a FlexTram train lit up in a yard at night, and closes on “On-site transportation, finally solved.”

The sign is the point. Fans know roughly where the gate is. What they lack is a route. Named lines and numbered stops turn a big property into something a first-time visitor can read, the same way a city transit map does. We wrote more about that idea in Cities solved this 100 years ago.
